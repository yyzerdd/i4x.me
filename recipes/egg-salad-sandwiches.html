Ingredients

  • 1 lb Main Ingredient
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil or Butter
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • Fresh herbs for garnish

Instructions

  1. Prepare and chop all ingredients before heating.
  2. Heat oil over medium-high heat in a large pan or heavy pot.
  3. Cook primary ingredients for 8–10 minutes until browned.
  4. Season to taste and serve immediately while hot.

Notes

Storage: Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
